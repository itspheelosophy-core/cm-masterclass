FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you enroll.

Who is this Masterclass actually built for?▼
This Masterclass is built for three people: virtual assistants and creator economy project managers who are already doing campaign-adjacent work and need the positioning and frameworks to step into a CM role; entry-level creator assistants who support creators or talent agencies and want to grow their operational scope; and entry-level campaign managers who are in the role but don't yet have a system behind how they run campaigns.
Do I need influencer marketing experience to enroll?▼
No prior influencer marketing experience required. The Masterclass starts at the foundation — the creator economy ecosystem, how deals work, who the stakeholders are. If you have VA or PM experience, you already have more than you think. This course builds the creator-specific layer on top of it.
What makes this different from other creator economy courses?▼
Most creator economy courses teach content strategy or audience growth. This one teaches operations. The phase-by-phase campaign lifecycle, the email thread simulations with tooltips, the campaign tracker, the practice simulations everything is built around what it actually looks like to run a campaign from the creator side, not market one.
What is the white-label contractor pathway?▼
FCG-certified graduates have the opportunity to be matched with real campaign work through For Creator Good's contractor network. This isn't a job guarantee, it's a pipeline for graduates who are ready to deploy their skills on real client campaigns, under FCG's operational framework.
What's the difference between the two tiers?▼
Both tiers include the full Masterclass, supporting Playbook, and the FCG certification pathway. The Masterclass + Mentorship tier adds practice simulations, membership mentorship, and priority access to the white-label contractor placement pipeline.
